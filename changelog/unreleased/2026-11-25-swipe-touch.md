- **A swipe hears a finger land.** `El::on_swipe_touch(f)`, beside `El::on_swipe`, calls `f(state, true, tx)` the moment a finger lands in the element, before it has moved far enough to be a swipe, so a slide still settling can stop under the finger; `f` hears `false` when that finger lifts without swiping (a tap, a vertical pan, a second finger), and a finger that does swipe lifts as the swipe's `Up`. A finger on something inside that drags itself is not heard.
