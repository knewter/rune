- A long press in a `text_input` selects the word under the finger, as a double click does, and moving the finger extends it by words; a focused field's selected range is the selection `Cx::selection_rects`, `Cx::selection_clip` and `Runtime::selection_handles` read when no `El::selectable` text is selected (`Cx::selection_in_field` tells which), so its handles are drawn and taken like a transcript's. A finger on a handle moves that end along the line the handle hangs from, held by the offset it took the handle at, rather than the line under the finger.
