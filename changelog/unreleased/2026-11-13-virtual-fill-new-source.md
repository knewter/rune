- **Virtual lists: a list showing new data fills on its own budget.** When an index-built list's rows are replaced by rows that keep none the reader could see (another conversation, another folder), or another list mounts in its place, while the old rows' fill was still waiting or had given up, the new rows now start their own fill at once. They used to inherit the old fill's wait and its spent resumes, so the new rows could stay blank until a scroll. A list mounting or unmounting elsewhere in the window also starts a fresh fill.
