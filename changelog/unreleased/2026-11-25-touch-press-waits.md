- **A finger shows no hover, and a press inside a scroll, swipe or drag shows only once the finger rests.** As Android's views (`ViewConfiguration.getTapTimeout()`) and UIKit's scroll views (`delaysContentTouches`) do, a control a finger lands on inside something that may take the gesture shows pressed after the tap timeout (100 ms, or the host's: `Runtime::set_tap_timeout`); a finger that leaves the slop first shows no press at all, a tap that lifts sooner still presses and flashes its press as it lets go, and where nothing above can take the gesture the press shows at once. A finger never takes an element's hover look. The touch slop is the host's too (`Runtime::set_touch_slop`, 8 points without it). Only how a press looks waits: its handlers, focus and a drag's `Phase::Down` do not. The mouse is unchanged.
