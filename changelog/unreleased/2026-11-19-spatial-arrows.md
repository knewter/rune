- Focus: `Runtime::set_spatial_arrows` makes arrow keys move focus to the nearest focusable control drawn that way, as a television remote's D-pad does (Android's `FocusFinder` choice: the shared row or column first, then the gap and the offset). The first arrow focuses the first control, an edge passes the key on, a single-line field gives up only Up and Down, and a menu's highlight keeps its own arrows. Off by default; keyboard arrows still walk their group.
